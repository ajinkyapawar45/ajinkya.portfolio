Final-year project · In development

Design and Development of a Tractor-Mounted PTO-Driven Grape Root-Removal Machine

Developing a tractor-mounted, PTO-driven machine for removing grapevine roots and stumps from agricultural fields. The proposed design uses a rotating ring assembly and two opposing conical gripping/cutting blades to grip and remove grapevine roots. Power is transmitted from the tractor PTO through a gearbox to rotate the cutter assembly, while a separate hydraulic mechanism supports blade operation and lifting. The design focuses on effective root removal, suitable tractor mounting, adequate vertical clearance, simple construction, and cost-effective fabrication. The project aims to reduce manual effort and improve efficiency in agricultural root-removal operations.
